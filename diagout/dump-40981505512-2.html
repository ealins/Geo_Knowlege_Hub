Welcome

We are pleased to invite you to the 18th International 3DGeoInfo Conference 2023, which will take place in Munich on the 13th and 14th of September 2023. The conference will be preceded on the 12th of September by a full-day workshop called “International Forum on Urban Digital Twins” organized by the City of Munich in cooperation with the Technical University of Munich.

The 18th 3DGeoInfo conference is hosted by the Chair of Geoinformatics at the Technical University of Munich.

If you have questions or are interested in sponsoring contact 3dgeoinfo.gis@ed.tum.de

Note: The conference will take place in the week before the famous Oktoberfest. You may want to stay a week longer in Munich. In any case, we recommend booking your accommodation early. For accommodation recommendations in and around Munich-Garching, please check here.

Themes for the Conference

  • 3D/4D (city) modelling and visualization
  • 3D/4D data acquisition, processing, management, and integration
  • Geo-information standards and GIS-BIM interoperability / GeoBIM
  • 3D indoor and outdoor navigation
  • Applications of 3D/4D Geoinformation such as simulations and analyses
  • 3D/4D interfaces such as virtual, mixed, and augmented reality
  • 3D/4D geospatial big data and machine learning
  • Smart Cities and 3D/4D Urban Digital Twins

Program Overview

12. September 2023

International Urban Digital Twin Forum

@ Munich Urban Colab

The digital forum is co-organized and sponsored by the City of Munich.

The forum is a day’s event focusing on the latest developments in the field of Digital Twins followed by an ice-breaking session. 

For more details, please check the International Forum webpage.

13-14. September 2023

18th 3DGeoInfo Conference

@Technical University of Munich, Garching (Garching Forschungszentrum)

The 3DGeoInfo conference aims to bring together researchers, practitioners, and professionals from academia, industry, and government to discuss the latest advances in 3D geoinformation science and technology.

A detailed program itinerary is available here and in the Call for Participation

Getting Around Munich

Find out what Munich has to offer, the sights to see, accommodation recommendations, and how to travel around the city by public transportation. Check more on the venues here

Forum venue - Munich Urban Colab is located centrally in the city and can be reached by multiple means of public transport. 

Conference venue - Please note that TUM has multiple campuses and the conference is being organised at the TUM Garching campus (Garching Forschungszentrum) in the north or the city. The campus can be reached by public transportation U-bahn (subway line) U6 or by buses X230, 292 and 230.

Conference Dinner - The venue is also centrally located in the Marienplatz and can be easily reached by U-bahn (Subway), S-Bahn (Metro), Tram and buses from both Marienplatz and Karlsplatz (Stachus)

Registration Details

Registration to the conference is now available via the conference management system.**

Participants registered for the 3DGeoInfo Conference can also take part in the International Digital Twin Forum for free. The registration price includes participation in the International Forum on Digital Twins and the entire 3DGeoInfo Conference including the welcome reception, conference dinner, and proceedings. 

Note that early bird registration ends on 7 August 2023 10. August 2023.

  • Regular registration: Early-bird: 500€  (after 10. August 2023: 550€)
  • Student registration: Early-bird: 250€  (after 10. August 2023: 300€)

Members of organizing parties get a reduction of 30€.

**Please note that the Registration for the International Forum on Urban Digital Twins is now closed!
