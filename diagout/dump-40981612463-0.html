O3DM

Optical 3D Metrology

18-20 Nov 2026
Braunschweig, Germany

The primary theme of O3DM is the precise 3D measurement and accurate modelling from imaging and range sensors.
O3DM topics of interest include:

  • sensor investigations and characterisation
  • radiometric and geometric calibration of sensors
  • sensor/data fusion
  • quality and calibration of colour for 3D models acquired by optical and range sensing techniques
  • algorithms for precise 3D data derivation and processing
  • ambiguous sequences in image orientation
  • hybrid adjustment
  • AI methods in metrology and industrial inspection
  • handling of transparent or reflective surfaces
Braunschweig
Germany

Low-Cost 3D

9th International ISPRS Workshop Low-Cost 3D-Sensors, Algorithms, Applications

18-20 Nov 2026
Braunschweig, Germany

Themes of Event

  • Image- and range-based low-cost sensor systems including low-cost scanning systems
  • Low-cost sensor calibration and system integration
  • Accuracy investigations in low-cost sensor systems
  • Low-cost sensors for automatic data acquisition
  • Automation in data registration
  • Point cloud analysis
  • Image matching and 3D reconstruction
  • Low-cost sensors including open-source and free algorithms for terrestrial and UAV 3D modeling
  • Web-based tools for terrestrial 3D modeling
  • 4D modelling
  • Low-cost systems for Virtual and Augmented Reality applied to the visualization of 3D models
  • Low-cost solutions for 3D printing
Braunschweig
Germany

SWS Vienna GREEN 2026 – Green Science for Green Life

International multidisciplinary scientific conference

02-05 Dec 2026
Vienna, Austria

Venue: Schönbrunn Palace, 1130 Vienna, Austria

The conference programme includes the special session “From Urban Data to Sustainable Decisions: AI, Climate and the Built Environment” on 3 December 2026. Scientific partner of this session: IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society (GRSS) Austrian Chapter. The session connects AI, Earth observation, urban analytics, climate adaptation, energy efficiency and the sustainable built environment.

Current deadlines:
abstracts, early-bird registration, panel/workshop proposals and full papers — 16 November 2026
poster PDFs — 23 November 2026

Official conference: https://sgemviennagreen.org/

Current deadlines: https://sgemviennagreen.org/deadlines-programme.php

Organiser: SWS Scholarly Society / Secretariat Bureau
