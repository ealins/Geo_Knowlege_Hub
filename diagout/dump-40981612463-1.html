PCP 2027

5th EuroSDR Workshop on Point Cloud Processing

04-05 Feb 2027
Stuttgart, Germany

Dear Colleagues,

We cordially invite you to the 5th International Workshop on Point Cloud Processing in Stuttgart, Germany on February 4-5, 2027.

Following the previous editions, EuroSDR again brings together experts from industry, academia and national mapping agencies to present and discuss the processing and automatic interpretation of area covering point clouds focusing on mapping purposes. Themes of the event include hardware developments, acquisition of point cloud data and its visualization, intelligent generation of training data, Multi-Modal data processing of LiDAR data and imagery, semantic segmentation and classification as well as applications and use-cases from practice. 

The program will again feature invited presentations, complemented by oral and poster presentations selected from submitted abstracts. Researchers and practitioners interested in contributing are invited to submit a 1000-word abstract to pcp2027@ifp.uni-stuttgart.de by November 1, 2026.

Further information on the workshop including registration, venue and program updates is available at https://pcp.ifp.uni-stuttgart.de

See you in Stuttgart!

Norbert Haala, Markus Englich, Fabio Remondino and George Vosselman

Stuttgart
Germany
pcp2027@ifp.uni-stuttgart.de

60th Photogrammetric Week

06-09 Apr 2027
Stuttgart, Germany

Advancement in Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Geoinformatics

The Institute for Photogrammetry and Geoinformatics of the University of Stuttgart and the OpenPhoWo partners would like to inform about the

60th Photogrammetric Week (PhoWo) in Stuttgart
from Tuesday, April 6 to Friday, April 9, 2027.

The biennial event was initiated by Carl Pulfrich as a “vacation course in photogrammetry” in 1909; since 1973 it has been held at the University of Stuttgart. Over the years, remote sensing and geoinformatics together with photogrammetry have become the major subject areas of this conference. Today, the Photogrammetric Week enjoys international recognition as an upgrading seminar and a platform for the exchange of experience.

The lectures will focus on trends and achievements of the following topics:

  • Sensors and Data Acquisition
  • Photogrammetry, Computer Vision and Remote Sensing
  • Geoinformatics

The presentations will be in English. Sufficient time will be allocated for discussions.

The programme will be supplemented with demonstrations and comments on practical examples in the afternoons. The fringe events will offer sufficient opportunity for personal contacts.

We look forward to meeting you in Stuttgart.

 

Stuttgart, June 2025

Prof. Dr.-Ing. Uwe Sörgel
Institute for Photogrammetry and Geoinformatics
University of Stuttgart

Stuttgart
Germany
phowo@ifp.uni-stuttgart.de

XXVII International Multidisciplinary Scientific GeoConference SGEM 2027

03-12 Jul 2027
Albena, Bulgaria

Venue: Flamingo Grand Congress Centre, Albena Resort & Spa, Bulgaria

The conference's geospatial scope includes geoinformatics, GIS, cartography, photogrammetry, remote sensing and geospatial technologies, within a broader multidisciplinary programme of Earth and planetary sciences, geology, mining, water, ecology, climate, soils, forestry, energy and environmental technologies. The working language is English, with paid on-site and virtual participation options.

Abstracts: 22 March 2027
Workshop proposals: 25 May 2027
Full papers: 15 June 2027
Early-bird registration/payment: 22 March 2027
Regular payment: 11 May 2027
Official deadlines: https://www.sgem.org/dates-deadlines

The preliminary programme provides registration on 3–4 July, scientific sessions on 5–11 July, and a conditional post-conference field trip on 12 July. Exact daily hours and the final programme will be confirmed later.

Albena
Bulgaria
SWS Scholarly Society / Secretariat Bureau
