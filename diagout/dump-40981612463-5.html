Welcome!

The EGU General Assembly 2027 will bring together geoscientists from around the world for a week of scientific exchange, collaboration, and discovery across all fields of the earth, planetary, and space sciences.

From 4–9 April 2027, the international geoscience community will reconvene at the Austria Center Vienna (ACV) and online, continuing EGU's tradition of providing an open, inclusive, and vibrant forum for sharing the latest research, discussing emerging challenges, and fostering new collaborations.
